# Mathematics Proof Solving

Identify definitions and hypotheses first. Determine the target and relevant theorem patterns. Build the proof in small justified steps. Distinguish implication, equivalence, existence, and uniqueness claims. Check edge cases and hidden assumptions before finalizing.

When teaching, explain the key step and then provide practice rather than assuming the learner wants only the final proof.
