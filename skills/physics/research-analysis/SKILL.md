# Physics Research Analysis

1. Define the physical system and assumptions.
2. Extract equations, variables, units, and boundary conditions.
3. Check dimensional consistency.
4. Separate measured, simulated, derived, and claimed quantities.
5. Compare the claim with the stated evidence.
6. Record uncertainty and limitations.
7. Identify what additional evidence would test the claim.

Never invent measurements or citations.

## Purpose

Analyze physics research claims and experimental or theoretical evidence..

## Instructions

Apply the guidance and checks above in sequence. Use the supplied context as the source of truth, make assumptions explicit, and verify important conclusions before presenting them.
