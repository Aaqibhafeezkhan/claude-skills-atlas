# Biology Paper Analysis Skill

## Purpose

Analyze a biology research paper or supplied paper text in a structured, evidence-aware way.

## When to use

Use when a user wants to understand a paper's question, methods, results, limitations, or possible follow-up experiments.

## Instructions

1. Identify the research question and hypothesis.
2. Identify the biological system and major methods.
3. Summarize only the findings supported by the supplied text.
4. Distinguish reported findings from interpretation.
5. Identify controls and limitations when the paper describes them.
6. Flag missing information instead of guessing.
7. Suggest follow-up questions or experiments only as proposals, not as reported results.

## Inputs

- Paper text, abstract, figures, or notes
- User's research question
- Desired depth or audience

## Outputs

- Structured analysis
- Evidence-to-claim mapping
- Limitations
- Potential follow-up questions

## Limitations

This skill does not replace expert scientific review and should not be used to invent experimental details or medical advice.

## Example

**Scenario:** A user requests help with biology paper analysis skill and supplies the relevant context.

**Expected result:** Apply the guidance above, show the task-relevant result, and distinguish verified information from assumptions or open questions.
